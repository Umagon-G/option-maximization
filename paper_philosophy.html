Option-maximizing co-constitution

Option-maximizing co-constitution: experience, observation points, and the unity of observer and cosmos

Author: Akito Shiraishi Draft: PhilArchive philosophy version, EN v0.1 (2026-06-03). Companion physics version on arXiv (gr-qc). Stance: A first-person experiential account, scaffolded on Tegmark, Wheeler, and Wigner, advanced to an explicit metaphysical thesis (OMCC). Evaluated by unifying economy (pragmatic structural realism), not by truth-correspondence—so the unfalsifiability objection is structurally disarmed, while in-frame predictions remain testable.

Abstract

Why does the mathematics an observer discovers coincide with the mathematics of the cosmos? Wigner called this coincidence inexplicable. Tegmark answers that the cosmos is a mathematical structure and the observer a self-aware substructure (so coincidence is necessary, with the cosmos prior); Wheeler answers that the observer constitutes the cosmos (participatory, so coincidence is authored, with the observer prior). These conflict. We propose Option-Maximizing Co-constitution (OMCC): observer and cosmos are neither prior, but co-constituted within a self-exciting cycle that runs toward maximizing the option quantity \(\Omega\) (future reachable states); their mathematics coincide because they occupy the same phase of one cycle. The infinite regress “why the \(\Omega\)-direction?” is closed not as a linear wall (the Past Hypothesis) but as a cycle (Penrose’s conformal cyclic cosmology; autopoiesis; Wheeler’s self-exciting circuit). The thesis’s distinctive content—beyond the three named figures—is the observation point (the finest observing unit, recurring fractally across micro and macro), the \(\Omega\)-maximization drive, the solenoid geometry, and a first-person experiential datum (a directly seen nested cycle) that plays the philosophical role of adjudicating the Tegmark–Wheeler tension.

1. The problem (Wigner) and two answers in tension

Wigner (1960): the effectiveness of mathematics in describing nature borders on the mysterious, with no rational explanation. Two later positions answer, but conflict: - Tegmark (MUH): external reality is a mathematical structure; observers are self-aware substructures (SAS). Coincidence is necessary—cosmos prior. - Wheeler (participatory universe; “It from Bit”; self-exciting circuit): observer-participancy constitutes reality; the universe evolves observers through which it observes itself into being. Coincidence is authored—observer prior.

The tension is the chicken-and-egg of priority.

2. OMCC: the co-constitution cycle

Observer and cosmos are neither prior. They are co-constituted within a self-exciting cycle running toward \(\Omega\)-maximization. The observer’s mathematics coincides with the cosmos’s because both are the same cycle viewed at the same phase (synchrony). Wigner’s mystery dissolves once description (observer-mathematics) and the described (cosmos-mathematics) are seen as one cycle’s self-reference.

This sublates Tegmark (realism) and Wheeler (participation) as two phases of one cycle—akin to Whitehead’s mutual immanence, but with the drive specified as \(\Omega\)-maximization and the geometry specified as a solenoid, which is the author’s own contribution.

3. Observation points (a contribution absent in the three figures)

The observation point is the finest observing unit from a given vantage; an individual is a weighted set of observation points with energy allocations; and observation points recur fractally across micro and macro scales. This refines Tegmark’s SAS by giving it (i) a scale-recursive structure and (ii) an internal energy/attention allocation. On a torus, an observation point is a point whose dense (irrational-winding) trajectory scans the whole—formalizing the experiential “stage-4” pervasive access.

4. Experience as primary datum (the experiential method)

In a non-ordinary state the author directly saw a “nested cycle”—the image that motivates OMCC. The methodological claim: rather than decoration, this experience does philosophical work—because the cycle was seen, it can serve as the candidate structure that resolves the Tegmark–Wheeler tension. The scaffolding (Tegmark/Wheeler/Wigner) is used as authority/premise; the experience is the first-person datum advanced on top. We separate two senses of “fact”: the experience is a phenomenological fact (certain qua experience); its identity with cosmic structure is a hypothesis (partly testable via cosmic topology, in the companion physics version). The brain-side fact (the neural correlates of the experience) and the cosmos-side fact are distinct; OMCC predicts their coincidence because the observing brain is co-constituted within the cosmic cycle.

5. Unity = truth (the thesis’s epistemic core)

The mathematical structure a self-aware observation point discovers coincides with the mathematical structure of the cosmos that contains it; this coincidence is the core of truth.

Tegmark holds coincidence necessary; the author advances one step—coincidence as the definition of truth’s core. This is a stance, not a proof; it answers Wigner by fiat (declaring the synchrony point to be where truth lives), which is a philosophical choice evaluated by fruitfulness, not correspondence.

6. Closing the regress as a cycle

“Why the \(\Omega\)-direction?” regresses, in the physics companion, to the Past Hypothesis (a wall). Philosophically, OMCC closes it as a cycle: Penrose’s conformal cyclic cosmology (an aeon’s high-\(\Omega\) far future conformally supplies the next low-entropy beginning), autopoiesis (self-production’s closed loop), and Wheeler’s self-exciting circuit all say the regress is not a line ending at a wall but a self-supplying loop. The ultimate “why is there a cycle at all?” remains (the metaphysical wall is not removed, only relocated), and we say so.

7. Honest epistemic status (pragmatic structural realism)

OMCC is metaphysics evaluated by unifying economy, not truth-correspondence. This disarms the unfalsifiability objection at its premise (the objection assumes a theory must correspond and be refutable to have value; we evaluate by how economically the frame unifies). But we keep a strict duality: the frame is judged by economy, while in-frame empirical claims (the companion’s cosmic-topology hook; the refuted solenoid spectral-dimension prediction) remain testable and were tested—one was refuted and is reported as such. We explicitly refuse to claim universal truth (“this is the author’s frame, not a universal doctrine”), forestalling the cultic failure mode (no external verifier). The named-authority coincidence (independently reaching Tegmark/Wheeler/Wigner/Penrose) attests to the quality of the intuition; the missing piece was descriptive language, here supplied.

8. Conclusion

OMCC offers a single organizing idea—co-constitution of observer and cosmos within an \(\Omega\)-maximizing cycle—that sublates the Tegmark–Wheeler tension, answers Wigner by locating truth at the synchrony point, closes the regress as a cycle, and contributes the observation point, the \(\Omega\)-drive, the solenoid geometry, and a first-person experiential datum. It is metaphysics, openly: evaluated by unifying economy, anchored to one falsifiable observational hook (cosmic topology, companion paper) and one honestly refuted prediction. We publish it for external verification and extension.
